# Scoring

## Hard disqualifiers

Drop the product, and say why, if any of these apply:

- Copies or uses a brand, logo, character or patented design (IP takedowns and frozen payouts).
- Regulated or claim-heavy: supplements, medical or health claims, cosmetics with active ingredients, baby food. Children's toys and electronics need safety certificates (CE, FCC, CPC, SASO in Saudi Arabia); flag them and only keep them if the supplier can provide the certificates.
- Hard to ship: lithium batteries, liquids, aerosols, magnets in bulk, blades, flammables, perishables.
- Net profit under 15% of the selling price at a realistic CPA, according to the calculator.
- Sold cheaply in every local supermarket or by Amazon itself at a price you can't beat.

## Rubric (100 points)

| Factor | Points | Full marks when | Low marks when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demand and trend | 20 | Rising or steady over 5 years, rising in the last 12 months | One spike then decline (fad), or flat at a low level |
| Proof of sales | 20 | Two or more independent signals: best-seller rank, thousands sold, ads running 30+ days, several stores featuring it | Only social buzz, or no sales evidence |
| Margin | 20 | Break-even ROAS of 1.8 or lower, net 25%+ at the expected CPA, landed cost at most a third of the price | Break-even ROAS above 3, or net under 15% |
| Competition | 15 | Few stores or ads, no dominant brand, room for a better angle | Many identical ads, price war, a big brand or Amazon dominates |
| Marketability | 10 | Solves a clear problem or has a "wow" you can show in a 3-second video; not easy to find in local shops | Hard to show on video, generic commodity |
| Logistics and risk | 15 | Light, small, unbreakable, no sizes, shipping under 10 days, low return risk | Heavy or bulky, fragile, many sizes or colors (returns), slow shipping |

Score each factor, sum to a total, and give one line of reasoning per factor with the evidence link.

## Verdict bands

| Total | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 80 to 100 | Test now |
| 65 to 79 | Test with a small budget and a sharp angle |
| 50 to 64 | Watch list: check again in 2 to 4 weeks |
| Under 50 | Skip |

## Sanity checks before recommending

- The selling price is at least 2.5 to 3 times the landed cost (product plus shipping) when you will pay for ads.
- The break-even CPA is above what similar products typically pay in that country and channel. If you don't know typical CPAs, state that plainly and give a range rather than a single number.
- At least one complaint in competitor reviews can be turned into a better offer or a stronger ad angle.
